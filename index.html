FAQ

What to know before you book

A few quick details that help venues and party hosts plan with confidence.

How far ahead should I book?

Reach out as soon as you have a target date. Weekend evenings and public venue nights tend to fill first.

What do you need on site?

Most events need a stable performance area, standard power, setup time before guests sing, and a plan for indoor or covered outdoor weather.

Can you host public venue nights?

Yes. Mic Drop Karaoke can support bars, restaurants, breweries, fundraisers, and recurring community events.

What affects the final price?

Timing, travel, guest count, setup complexity, event length, and any special audio or coordination needs can all affect the quote.
